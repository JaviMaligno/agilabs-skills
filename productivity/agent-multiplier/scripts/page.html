Kinds of work

When you worked in two categories at the same moment, that time is shared equally between them, so the attention column adds up to the total. Agent hours add up every worker: main agents, subagents, background tasks and remote runs. Inputs per hour divides your inputs by the hours when you or an agent were active. A sitting is a run of inputs with breaks of 30 minutes or less.
